# 00 · Course Roadmap: Business / Operations Analyst in 16 Weeks

> **Target roles (in priority order):** Business / Data Analyst · Operations Analyst · (secondary) Consulting / Strategy Analyst
> **Target industries:** E-commerce & quick commerce · Fintech · Logistics & supply chain · Manufacturing · Startups
> **Pace:** 2–3 hours a day, 7 days a week, with festival breaks built in
> **Goal:** a job-ready portfolio by Week 9, applications from Week 10–11, interview-ready by Week 16

---

## 1. The rules of this course

1. **This chapter list is the whole syllabus.** New tools appear on social media every week. Ignore them. If a tool is not in this roadmap, you do not need it to get hired. Mute accounts that make you anxious.
2. **Consistency beats intensity.** 2 focused hours every day is better than 6 hours once a week. If you miss a day, do the next day's work. Don't try to catch up everything at once. Each week has a catch-up buffer.
3. **Try first, then ask AI.** Attempt every assignment yourself for at least 15 minutes before asking AI. Use AI as a tutor and checker, not as an answer machine.
4. **Excel computes numbers; AI writes words.** Never copy a number from an AI chat into a report without checking it in Excel.
5. **Learn in Hindi if it helps, produce in English.** Videos can be Hindi/Hinglish. Everything you *write* (notes, emails, reports, posts) is in English. That is how the English improves.
6. **Honest portfolio.** RainCraft is a simulated company. Always describe these as *portfolio projects on simulated data*. Recruiters respect this. Claiming them as job experience backfires in interviews.

## 2. How a normal day looks (~2.5 hours)

| Block | Time | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Learn** | 60–75 min | Read the chapter notes, watch the linked videos (1.25× speed is fine) |
| **Do** | ~60 min | The daily assignment, on RainCraft data, saved to `my_work/` |
| **Case of the Day** | 15–20 min | A short real-company business question, answered in 5–8 lines |
| **English** | 10–15 min | Speaking or email practice (voice mode in Gemini/Claude works well) |

**Day 6** = Weekly Case Study (2 hrs). **Day 7** = Review quiz + GitHub upload + LinkedIn post + 30-minute check-in with your mentor.

## 3. The 16 weeks

### Phase 1: Foundations (Weeks 1–3)

| Week | Business & thinking | Tools | AI skill | Key deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | How companies work, order-to-cash, analyst roles, the P&L | Excel: Tables, $ references, SUMIFS / COUNTIFS / AVERAGEIFS, UNIQUE | Prompt structure, verifying AI numbers | Case: *"Where is our money?"* (receivables) |
| **2** | Strategy refresh: SWOT, PESTLE, Porter's Five Forces, 4Ps | Excel: data cleaning (TRIM, PROPER, text-to-columns, remove duplicates), XLOOKUP, IF/IFS, date functions, data validation | AI for formulas & cleaning steps | Clean the messy sales file; competitor analysis |
| **3** | KPIs: revenue, margin, AOV, DSO, OTIF, stock cover | Excel: Pivot Tables, charts, slicers, dashboard layout | AI for chart choice & dashboard narration | 🏆 **Capstone 1** |

### Phase 2: Business & operations depth (Weeks 4–6)

| Week | Business & thinking | Tools | AI skill | Key deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **4** | Finance: balance sheet, cash flow, working capital, receivables ageing, break-even, unit economics | Excel: Power Query (import, clean, append, refresh) | AI to explain financial statements | Working-capital model |
| **5** | Operations: supply chain, inventory (ABC, EOQ, safety stock, reorder point, dead stock), Lean, 5 Whys, fishbone | draw.io process maps, Excel what-if | AI to draft process maps & SOPs | Inventory health analysis |
| **6** | Structured problem solving: MECE, issue trees, hypotheses, case interviews, guesstimates | PowerPoint: storyline, SCQA, slide design | AI as a case-interview sparring partner | 🏆 **Capstone 2** |

### Phase 3: Data tools (Weeks 7–9)

| Week | Business & thinking | Tools | AI skill | Key deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| **9** | Correlation, sampling, A/B testing intuition, forecasting (moving average, seasonality) | SQL + Excel | AI to explain statistical results in plain English | 🏆 **Capstone 3** · Resume v1 · LinkedIn profile overhaul |

### Phase 4: Visualisation & storytelling (Weeks 10–12)

| Week | Business & thinking | Tools | AI skill | Key deliverable |

### Phase 5: Business Analysis, AI & job launch (Weeks 13–16)

| Week | Business & thinking | Tools | AI skill | Key deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
